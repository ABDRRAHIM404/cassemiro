## Direction: built with intention

Charcoal surfaces, mineral ivory text and carefully limited bronze accents. Upright editorial serif display typography plus a self-hosted modern sans for navigation, controls and body copy. No repeated italic split headlines, giant empty white bands or generic feature cards. Text remains comfortably readable and the layout has a consistent, compact scale on small laptops and phones.

Four signature compositions:

1. Cinematic construction opening with a calm, left-aligned caption and functional phase rail. Photography remains dominant; both primary actions fit in the first viewport.
2. Full-height founder portrait integrated into an asymmetric editorial composition; the verified 43+ years anchors the narrative, not invented project counts.
3. Photographic trust experience with one featured commitment and accessible secondary controls. No four equal static columns or gratuitous nested borders.
4. Oversized, stationary project catalogue with restrained side previews and a clear caption/navigation band. Sparse real content is intentional, never duplicated to suggest a larger portfolio.

Two motion cues: image reveal/very subtle depth on entry; interruptible user-driven catalogue and trust transitions. Respect reduced motion and never add an uncontrolled decorative loop. Preserve the proven frame renderer rather than replacing it for stylistic consistency.
