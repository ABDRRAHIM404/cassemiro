# Content guide

Write visitor-facing content in Brazilian Portuguese. Keep the tone direct, experienced, calm and specific.

- Use only confirmed facts, real projects and authorized customer testimonials.
- Say that Sérgio has more than 43 years of practical experience; do not claim the company is 43 years old.
- Do not invent prices, certifications, project locations, durations or client names.
- Keep project summaries concise and put the full story in the description.
- Describe images for accessibility without keyword stuffing.
- Leave unknown CNPJ and social links blank; the site hides them automatically.
- Keep public pricing hidden unless the business explicitly approves the displayed wording.

After major copy changes, review desktop and mobile layouts because editorial headings are intentionally large.
